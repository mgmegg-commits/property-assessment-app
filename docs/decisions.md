# Design decisions made during the build

Decisions agreed with Cecil live in the Decisions log in `docs/spec.md`. This file records design choices made while building that the spec doesn't cover. Newest first.

"Plan 12.x" refers to the numbered decisions in section 12 of `docs/archive/plan-2026-11-25.md`. **Provisional** means we're building on it now, and it may change once confirmed.

| Date | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Only wrong passwords count toward the sign-in limit** (10 a minute from one address, then a one-minute wait). v1 counted every attempt, right or wrong. | A shared office address, or the automated tests, signing in correctly many times shouldn't lock anyone out. Guessing is still limited exactly as before. |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **The Follow-up and phone pages use v1's fonts (Archivo, IBM Plex Sans) served by the app itself** (`@fontsource` packages), not from Google Fonts. | Those pages keep the strict "the app's own files only" policy, and a phone on a weak signal doesn't wait on another site |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Until Branding & settings is saved, the follow-up pages use v1's own defaults:** the company "Cecil Ingram Properties LLC", and the latest issued report's "Prepared by" as the suggested name. | v1's page does the same, so both show the same names |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Each item's position is its place in the report's action list** (A1 first). The Follow-up page, the phone page and the export all list items in that order. | The order Cecil chose for the report (report, priority or due date) carries into follow-up |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **The Follow-up page edits an item in place:** tapping a row opens its facility, assigned person, due date, status buttons and a note, with its history. On a phone the table becomes one card per item. | The prototype shows the table but no editing; fewest clicks, and it must work at iPhone width |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **The Follow-up page picks a property with one-tap buttons** when there's more than one; its export covers the chosen property. | Items are "by property"; buttons rather than a dropdown (CLAUDE.md) |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Photos on the phone page come only through the link** (`/api/link/<token>/files/<id>`), and only those of that property's open items and their updates. A photo sent from a link is shrunk on the phone (at most 1600 px) and can only be attached by that link. | A link sees nothing beyond its property's open items |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **The simplified build replaces the Nov 25 milestones** (`docs/plan.md`). The Nov 25 plan is archived whole in `docs/archive/plan-2026-11-25.md`, so the "Plan 12.x" references below still resolve; its sections 1 to 9 stay as the long-term design. | Michael: the plan was too big for the beta with a skilled nursing operator (45 sites) |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **v1's page is served as built from the pinned commit, plus one script tag** before `</body>` that adds a "Follow-up" button next to "Branding & settings" on v1's home page. The file in `apps/v1-web` is never changed, and a test checks the served page is that file plus the tag. Its content security policy allows v1's inline code (by hash), its CDN libraries (cdnjs, jsDelivr) and Google Fonts. | "Keep v1's screens exactly as they are", and "linked from v1's home page". v1 loads its PDF, Excel, photo and font libraries from those CDNs, so they stay. |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Deleting a report hides it** (it disappears from the list and answers 404) rather than removing it. | "Reports and photos kept forever" |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **A property is identified by the property name on its issued report.** Follow-up items, links and the export are grouped by it. | v1 has no property record; the name is what Cecil types and what prints |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **The phone page lists only Open and In progress items.** The prototype also showed Done items; the build instructions say "only that property's open items". An item marked Done leaves the list, and "Thanks, your update was saved" confirms it. | The instructions override the prototype where they differ |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **The office's "who":** the Follow-up page asks for your name once on each device (it starts as the report settings' "Prepared by") and records it with every change. | One shared password can't say who made a change |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **A personal link is a long random token kept in the database**, so the Follow-up page can copy it again later. Anyone with the database can see the links, as they can see everything else. | Fewest clicks: a lost link doesn't need a new one |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Links use the copy's public address (`PUBLIC_URL`).** `./start.sh` sets it to the Mac's HTTPS address for phones; without it, links use the address the Follow-up page was opened at. | On the Mac the office uses http://localhost:8080, which a phone can't open |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **The Excel export keeps v1's tracker columns first** (Facility Name, Assessment Date, Action Item Description, Q1 to Q4, Facility Contact), then adds Status, Assigned To, Due Date, Facility and Last Update. The current quarter's column holds the latest update. | The tracker pastes into MPT-style sheets as before, and "with their current status" |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Facility contacts can set only In progress and Done.** The Follow-up page sets Open, In progress, Done or Deferred. Only a revision sets Withdrawn. | Matches the prototype's two buttons; Deferred is the client's call |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **The React start page moves to `/status`**, as a status page (app, database, version). | `/` is v1's page now |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Schema 7 moves a v1 report's assignee and due date to `finding.v1FollowUp`** instead of dropping them. Importing a report (step 2.10) turns them into the action item's starting values. Until then, sorting by due date and merging findings read them as v1 did. | "Old data always opens": nothing an earlier version saved is lost, and a migrated report sorts as it did in v1 |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Due dates reach the report model from outside.** `listActions(a, followUp)` takes the live action items' assignee and due date by finding id, falling back to `v1FollowUp`. | The model stays pure, and the report holds nothing the assessor doesn't set (A-12) |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **v1's `migrateAssessment()` became one step per schema (2 to 6).** They're tested against a word-for-word copy of v1's function on every fixture. | Numbered steps are what the migration rule asks for. The only difference is in theory: v1 re-ran its schema 3 and 4 clean-ups on documents already past them, which changes nothing on any document v1 saved. |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Transcripts have their own schema numbers.** v1's had none (counts as 1); schema 2 adds `leftOut` to each remark. The plan listed this under the assessment's step 7. | A transcript is a separate document, so it gets its own migrations |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **v1's tests are ported as JavaScript (`packages/core/test/v1/*.test.mjs`)** with their bodies word for word. Four that schema 7 changes are marked `Changed in v2` with the reason. | A diff against v1 shows only import lines, so "nothing weakened" can be checked |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Ten v1 tests wait for the step that brings their code** (drafting in 2.8, exports in 2.9), rather than bringing that code in early. Two of them are covered meanwhile by stand-ins with drafted values marked by hand. | The step 2.2 scope excludes drafting and exports |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Seed data lives in `packages/core/src/data/*.json`**: both templates with their standard sentences, the priority schemes, the 21 phrases, the disclaimer and the tracker columns. It's exported as `@crb/core/data/*.json`. The sentence functions take a template's own sentences and use v1's by default. | Later steps load these into the database. Standard sentences belong to a template (spec, Data model). |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Report settings start with an empty company and preparer, and have no `keepOriginals`.** | In v2 these come from the organization and its users; originals are always kept (Plan 12.15) |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **New reports no longer carry v1's `_by`** (which browser tab saved it). IDs inside the report (findings, photos) keep v1's short form; database rows use UUIDs. | v2 detects edits from another device with the row's version |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **No Zod schema for the document yet.** The migration tests check the shape by hand. The plan's "validate against the Zod schema" and "build its PDF" checks come with the API (2.3) and the PDF (2.9). | No new dependency for core in this step |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **Noted for step 2.3:** the model's "today" (a new report's date, the once-a-day version) uses the computer's local date, as v1 did in the browser. On a server that's UTC, so the server must pass the organization's local date. | A late-evening edit in Alabama would otherwise count as the next day |
| Nov 26, 2026 | **`.gitignore` ignores `/data/` and `/uploads/` at the top of the repo only.** | The bare `data/` rule also hid `packages/core/src/data` |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Database changes are hand-written, numbered SQL files** (`apps/server/migrations/0001_….sql`), applied in order by a small runner of our own when the app starts, each in a transaction. The app refuses to start against a database changed by a newer version of itself. Drizzle (from step 2.3) is used only for typed queries, not to generate migrations. | Row-level security, permissions and append-only rules need hand-written SQL in any case, so the SQL files are the one source of truth. A reviewer can read each change. |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **A copy on a Mac serves three addresses:** the app at `http://localhost:8080` for the Mac only (published on 127.0.0.1); the app over HTTPS at `https://<Mac>.local:8443` and the Mac's IP address for iPads and iPhones; and a set-up page at `http://<Mac>.local:8081` with the certificate to trust. Caddy makes its own certificate authority (`local_certs`). Each device trusts it once. | Safari on iPhone and iPad needs HTTPS for Face ID sign-in (step 2.3) and for offline features. The Mac's browser treats localhost as secure already. |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Test data is loaded only into a development copy with an empty database** (`APP_ENV=development`, `LOAD_FIXTURES=1`), from `tests/fixtures/seed`, mounted read-only. A production copy refuses `LOAD_FIXTURES=1`, and the image never contains test data. | Test data can never mix with a real copy's data |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **The public health check (`/api/health`) holds no data.** A development-only `/api/dev/summary` lists the test organizations for the start page; it doesn't exist in a production copy, and a test checks that. | There are no accounts until step 2.3, so nothing about any organization may be visible in a real copy |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Record IDs are random UUIDs** generated by PostgreSQL | Not guessable, URL-safe, and standard |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Every response carries strict security headers:** a content security policy allowing only the app's own files, no framing, `nosniff`, and `noindex`. The web build inlines nothing. | No CDNs (plan section 3). Reports are private. |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **TypeScript 6.0, not 7.0.** Playwright is pinned to 1.63.0. | typescript-eslint doesn't support TypeScript 7 yet. Playwright 1.63 matches the browsers preinstalled in Claude Code's sessions, and CI installs its own. |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Whose Claude account drafting uses** (Plan 12.20). Cecil's copy uses Cecil's own Anthropic account. A client copy uses the client's own account: an Anthropic API key, or Claude through their Bedrock, Vertex AI or Foundry account. Cecil's key is used in a client copy only with the client's written OK. Until Cecil's copy goes live, real drafting tests on Michael's Mac use Michael's own API key, set in that Mac's `.env` and never committed. | Data goes from the client's server straight to the AI provider under the client's own contract, and the AI bill is theirs |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Hosting: build once, install anywhere** (Plan 12.1). One package keeps each organization's data separate. A client gets either its own organization in Cecil's copy or the same package on its own server. | It keeps "one copy per client" possible without betting on it. The app needs organizations and roles in any case. |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **The package must be portable** (Plan 12.17). The same Docker Compose package runs on Michael's Mac (Docker Desktop, one start command, with fixture data), Cecil's computer, a managed host or a small cloud server. There's no staging server for now. Hosting for Cecil's real copy is chosen before he uses v2 for real. | Nothing needs paying for until Cecil uses it for real. Claude Code tests in its own sessions. |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Provisional: Cecil works inside a client's own copy** as an assessor user, rather than drafting in his copy and sending reports across (Plan 12.2). To confirm with Cecil's IT contact before Milestone 4. | One source of truth and no syncing between copies |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Tenants sign in with personal secure links**, with no password (Plan 12.3). Each link belongs to one named person, opens only that facility's items, is good once for 7 days, and signs the phone in for 90 days. There are no shared anonymous links. | No training needed. Every update has a who and when (F-4). Meets "no public links by default". |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Cecil and client staff sign in with an email link, then Face ID** (a passkey). Company single sign-on is optional per client copy (Plan 12.4). | No passwords for Cecil. Client IT can use its own sign-on. |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Provisional: action item statuses** are Open, Assigned, In progress, Done and Deferred (Plan 12.5). The facility or client marks Done. Only the client marks Deferred (with a reason) or changes due dates. Only the assessor records Fixed, Not fixed or Worse at the next visit, and Not fixed carries forward. To confirm with Cecil. | "Done" is a claim the next visit verifies (F-8) |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Issued means frozen report, live items** (Plan 12.6). Issuing stores the document and its PDF and never changes them. A revision is a new frozen copy. Its items match the earlier ones by finding: new findings add items, removed ones mark items Withdrawn, and live fields carry over. | The assessment stays as observed, and follow-up isn't lost when a revision is issued |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Drafting examples come only from the same client** unless Cecil marks one as shareable (Plan 12.7) | One client's report shouldn't be sent while drafting another's |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Provisional: a section's rating is the average of its subsections**, as in v1, and Cecil can override it (Plan 12.8). To confirm with Cecil. Switching to the worst rating is a small change. | Matches v1 and the reports Cecil approved |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **No third priority scheme for now** (Immediate / Short-term / Long-term). Schemes are data in the template, so one can be added later (Plan 12.9). | Cecil doesn't prefer it |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Overdue items are flagged on screen only in Milestone 3.** An opt-in weekly email digest comes later (Plan 12.10). | Fewer moving parts first. Email is still needed for sign-in links. |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Provisional: Cecil's questionnaires, templates and phrases belong to his firm.** They're installed into a client copy as a licensed pack that only assessor-firm admins can edit or export (Plan 12.11). Terms go to a lawyer later. | His question set is the "secret sauce" (Nov 23) |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **No v1 import and no "Export everything" button in v1** (Plan 12.12). Cecil has no real reports in v1. A plain JSON import stays, for loading test data (v1 backup JSON and fixtures). v1's document migrations 1 to 6 are still ported, so v1-shaped test data opens. | Nothing real to carry over |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Offline next-visit checklist on the iPad**, at the end of Milestone 3 or in Milestone 5 before February (Plan 12.13) | It's the one place Cecil works on site inside the app |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **One PR per step** of a milestone (the steps are in section 10 of `docs/archive/plan-2026-11-25.md`), with a commit per numbered item (Plan 12.14) | Smaller reviews. Milestone 2 alone would be a very large PR. |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Original photos are always kept**, alongside the 2048 px report copy (Plan 12.15) | Photos are kept forever. About 1 GB per visit. |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **F-1 moves into Milestone 2 and A-17 into Milestone 3** (Plan 12.16) | A-17 needs action items and their follow-up status, and F-1 creates the items at issue |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **The program coordinator view is deferred until after Milestone 3** (Plan 12.18). Every assessment records its assessor firm, so it can be added without a redesign. | F-10 is a "Could". The coordinator's needs are asked at next week's meeting. |
| Nov 25, 2026 | **Site videos are out of scope for the first release.** If wanted, they go in as a link in General comments (Plan 12.19). | Not in the spec. Videos would be large to store forever. |
