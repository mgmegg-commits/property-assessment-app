# Meeting with Cecil — Nov 24, 2026 (app review, notes)

Source: voice memo, 44 min (Apple transcript).

## Decisions
- **Details page approved as is:** property name, owner, use type, operator/tenant, facility contact, address, lat/long (averaged from photo GPS), date/time, prepared by, template, personnel, tax parcel ID, weather, general comments (paragraphs).
- **Priority labels:** either scheme per report. Switching converts High→Required, Medium/Low→Recommended. Cecil mentioned Immediate / Short-term / Long-term as an industry term set but doesn't prefer it.
- **Report order:** cover (details + photo) → executive summary → action items → body of report.
- **Action list sort:** a choice on the Report page: report order, priority, or due date.
- **Assignee and due date:** leave blank/TBD. Cecil identifies the problem; the client or tenant assigns and dates it.
- **Photo states:** In report (default) / Appendix only / Exclude, as one-click buttons side by side, not a dropdown. Also a real delete for bad shots.
- **One finding, many photos:** confirmed (e.g. water heater room shot + close-up + data plate; five photos of rotted eaves, one comment).
- **Same photo in two places:** rare but needed (e.g. missing exterior sealant shown again under interior damage).
- **Photo sorting workflow:** an All Photos tray docked at the bottom of every page to drag from; the photo viewer cycles all photos, shows where each is assigned, and lets him assign with one click.
- **Ratings layout:** Good / Fair / Poor / N/A buttons; the standard sentence directly under the selected rating, then a comment box. Same pattern at section level (Building Envelope) and subsection level (Roof).
- **Subsection controls:** a visible Delete button plus More (rename, move). No "Hide from this report".
- **Markup:** arrow, circle, numbered areas, text labels; red/yellow/white.
- **Fewer clicks everywhere** ("anything you can do is one less click").

## Big new direction: live follow-up instead of PDF + phone calls
- The client (who pays) can give tenants/facilities a live link, on the client's own secure server, to update action items.
- The client's asset team sees status and when each item was updated; missed due dates get flagged.
- Assignment chain: client → facility → person (e.g. MPT → a hospital → its maintenance tech).
- The assessment stays frozen as observed on the visit; a separate action tracker reads the latest assessment's action items and carries status, assignees, due dates and responses.
- Export for the client's work-order system (spreadsheet is fine).
- Next year Cecil verifies items marked "fixed" against what he sees.
- Replaces his quarterly calls and manual tracker.
